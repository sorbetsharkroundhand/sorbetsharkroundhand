## 🪴 The Herb Bed

### 🌱 [MINT](https://github.com/sorbetsharkroundhand/MINT) · *Mac Intelligent Note Taker*

A fully-local predictive writing engine for long-form Korean fiction.
Pause while typing and a ghost-text co-writer offers the next word or phrase —
one that has actually *read your novel*: characters, scenes, plot structure.
All inference happens on-device with Apple MLX. Nothing ever leaves your Mac.

`Swift` `SwiftUI` `Apple MLX` `Metal`

### 🌿 [SAGE](https://github.com/sorbetsharkroundhand/sage) · *Brewing data into insights*

Turns a pandas DataFrame into an **animated EDA story** — missing values,
distributions and correlations unfold scene-by-scene as a narrated video,
rendered with Manim. One line: `sage.report(df)` → `report.mp4`.

`Python` `pandas` `Manim` `TypeScript`

### 🍃 [sorbetsharkroundhand.github.io](https://github.com/sorbetsharkroundhand/sorbetsharkroundhand.github.io)

My personal plot of the internet — a site inspired by
[3b1b/manim](https://github.com/3b1b/manim)'s visual style.

`Astro` `TypeScript`
